# Long-session groundwork 0.9 — not a production two-hour workflow

Source only. Not compiled or device-tested. Existing short recorder, face effects and intro exporter remain unchanged. Main/export still show stale 10 GB usage labels; actual guard is configurable 350 GB. Fix those displays and long processing caps before recommending a new installer.

Adds an experimental service-owned CameraX recorder: planned maxima 30/60/120 minutes, front camera preferred, 1080p default, optional supported 4K, 3-second countdown, preview attachment, runtime camera/mic/notification permissions, declared camera+microphone foreground service, visible notification with Stop, timed partial wake lock, elapsed/file-size display, stop/finalize handling and atomic session-status journal. Service starts only in response to visible UI action; START_NOT_STICKY prevents automatic hidden restart.

Long mode removes the short recorder's fixed 2 GB cap. Instead it calculates a per-file ceiling from remaining managed budget and phone free space above reserve, keeps headroom, monitors every two seconds, and uses planned duration stop. Preflight estimates 20 Mbps for HD or 65 Mbps for requested 4K; encoder target is 16/50 Mbps but actual bitrate/device behavior may vary. A budget check is not a guaranteed disk reservation or hard quota. Header says device-selected HD for fallback, not verified actual dimensions; inspect saved recording through existing Details.

Records a SINGLE MP4 with a journal, NOT segmented crash-recoverable media. Journal has file name, status, elapsed time, bytes and last message, updated roughly every 10 seconds and at state transitions. It is recovery information, not MP4 repair or proof of a complete recording. Abnormal process death or power loss can still leave an unplayable MP4. Automatic resume, split recording, thermal/battery policy, safe long export recovery and finalized-file verification are not implemented.

Service lifecycle is independent of activity; activity detaches preview on leaving without requesting stop. Intended background/screen-off behavior requires actual Galaxy testing; manufacturer/system restrictions, notification denial, calls, camera contention, overheating or process termination can interrupt. Do not claim that background/two-hour recording is validated. Wake lock expires after planned duration plus two minutes and is released on normal finish/destruction.

Home blocks other recorder/export/budget actions while long mode reports active. This is UI-level coordination, not a comprehensive cross-process job lock. Journals queue to a single writer with AtomicFile; write errors currently not surfaced. Stop/finalize race behavior, bind/rebind, notification permissions, state after process recreation and destruction all require testing. Recorder orientation is not yet explicitly synchronized from the activity display; portrait/landscape output must be checked. Camera bind failure in 4K does not yet retry HD after a successful capability query.

Next work before recommending installation: compile/lint and fix; replace stale budget labels; long face-analysis processing/cache; remove export 2 GB ceiling safely; durable processing queue; explicit orientation and session recovery handling. Existing face analysis still rejects sources over 10 min; old export still cancels above 2 GB or on leaving screen. These limits are not removed by this commit.

After integration, test short long-mode recording and notification stop first, then background/reopen/lock-screen and orientation, storage simulations, 10 min, 30 min, 60 min, then 120 min with disposable material. Never fill disk to test. Do not trust important two-hour recordings until that validation is complete. No paid services, network permission, YouTube upload or automatic deletion added.
